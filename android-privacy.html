Privacy Policy — Ballpark Figure for Android

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Ballpark Figure ("Ballpark", and Ballpark on your home screen) is a weight tracker with a simple food log. This policy covers the Android app; the iPhone app has its own at https://ballpark.tombak.xyz/#privacy. The short version: your data stays on your phone, the app has no account and no server, and the developer cannot read any of it.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party software development kits that send data anywhere. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no server operated by the developer and no copy of your data the developer can reach.

What the app stores on your phone

All of the following stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Weight readings — the value, the date, and whether you typed it or it came from Health Connect.
  • Meals and snacks — which portion you tapped, the resulting calorie estimate, and when you logged it.
  • Waist and body-fat readings, if you record them.
  • Your settings — portion calibrations, goal, day patterns, daily target, reminder time, unit and colour scheme.

Home-screen widgets and Quick Settings tiles read the same storage; they run inside the app and send nothing anywhere.

Android backup

Android can back up apps' data to your own Google account, and can copy it to a new phone when you set one up. Ballpark allows this, so that replacing your phone does not lose your history. The backup is made by Android, not by the app, into your Google account; on phones with a screen lock it is end-to-end encrypted with that lock, so neither Google nor the developer can read it. The developer never has access to it. You can turn backup off in Settings › System › Backup (the exact path varies by phone).

Health Connect

If you grant permission, the app reads weight from Health Connect so that readings from a connected scale appear without retyping. It requests read access to weight only, and never writes to Health Connect.

Weight read from Health Connect is copied into the app's own storage so the app still works if you revoke permission later. It is used only to draw your trend and estimate your energy expenditure on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, never shared, never sold, and never used for advertising. The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect's own settings (Settings › Security & privacy › Privacy › Health Connect › App permissions, or the Health Connect app on older phones).

Notifications

If you switch on the weigh-in reminder, the app shows a local notification at the time you choose. If you type a weight into it, that weight is saved on your phone like any other. No notification passes through any server.

Links to other websites

The app contains a link to Open Food Facts, an open, community-run food database. Opening it hands a web address to your browser; nothing about your log, your weight or your device goes with it — at most the name of a meal slot, such as "lunch". That website's own privacy policy then applies.

Exporting your data

The app can export your records as CSV files. The file is written to the app's private cache and handed to Android's share sheet, where you choose what happens to it. The app uploads nothing.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. There are no advertisers, analytics providers or data brokers involved, and there is nothing for the developer to sell, because the developer has no copy.

Deleting your data

Within the app you can delete individual weights, meals, snacks and measurements, or everything at once (Settings › Delete all data). Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. A backup Android made to your Google account is managed in your Google account; weights in Health Connect stay there and are managed in Health Connect.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
